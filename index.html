Field Guide // 01

What you're holding

An electromagnetic frequency sensing circuit that lets you listen to the hidden electrical world - the way Bluetooth hardware sounds when it's transmitting, how a laptop touchpad clicks under your finger, the hum of a power supply behind a wall.

📡

Hear the Invisible

Inductors on the board pick up EMF in the 20Hz–20kHz range and amplify it ~1000x into your headphones - frequencies your ears couldn't otherwise catch.

🛠️

Build It Yourself

A guided through-hole soldering kit. No previous electronics experience required - every part is labeled, every step explained.

💡

Wear It, Light It

Once assembled, it's a glowing wearable badge on a lanyard - light your path at night, then go sniff out signals with headphones.
